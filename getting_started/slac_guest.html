SLAC Guest Accounts

Those without SLAC-approved federated identities or an existing SLAC Computing account can create a SLAC Guest account to access DESC resources at SLAC.

SLAC provides some documentation about setting up SLAC Guest accounts

On this page

  • Creating a SLAC Guest Account

  • Logging into SLAC Confluence with a SLAC Guest Account

  • Forgotten Password for SLAC Guest Accounts

Creating a SLAC Guest Account

When you receive an email from SLAC IT with the subject “Next Step: Link your federated account for SLAC access “, click the link in the email, and you will open a login page that looks like this:

Use the text box at the bottom where it says Enter Identity Provider Name to search for SLAC Guest.

Click SLAC Guest. This will bring up a login page:

Click Create Account.

Fill in the form, including the checkbox, and click Create.

Note

Make sure to enter your email address correctly with no typos. You can use any valid email address. Preferably this should be a permanent account you expect to have long-term access to (such as Gmail). Your email address will become your SLAC Guest account username.

Once you click Create you should see:

You will then receive an email from slac-iam@slac.stanford.edu with the subject “New Account Activation”. Click the link in that email to set your password in the web form:

When you have finished setting your password, click Activate Account. The following screen will appear and an email from slac-iam@slac.stanford.edu with the subject “Account Activated” will be sent to you:

Click Continue. You will be brought to a login screen — enter your new SLAC Guest account username (the email you provided when creating the account) and the password you just set.

Once logged in your should see this confirmation:

Logging into SLAC Confluence with a SLAC Guest Account

  1. Use the text box at the bottom where it says Enter Identity Provider Name to search for SLAC Guest.

  2. Click SLAC Guest. This will bring up a login page:

  3. Provide your SLAC Guest username and password and click Login.

  4. A two-factor authentication code will be sent to the email associated with your account.

Forgotten Password for SLAC Guest Accounts

Point your web browser to: https://confluence.slac.stanford.edu/display/LSSTDESC/Home

  1. Use the text box at the bottom where it says Enter Identity Provider Name to search for SLAC Guest.

  2. Click SLAC Guest. This will bring up a login page:

  3. Click Forgot Password?

  4. Enter your SLAC Guest account username (the email you used when creating the account) and click Send Email.
