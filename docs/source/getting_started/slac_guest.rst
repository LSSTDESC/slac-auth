SLAC Guest Accounts
====================

Those without SLAC-approved federated identities or an existing SLAC Computing
account can create a SLAC Guest account to access DESC resources at SLAC.

.. contents:: On this page
   :local:
   :depth: 1


Setting Up a SLAC Guest Account
---------------------------------

When you receive an email from SLAC IT with the subject **"Collaborator
Invitation to SLAC resources (LSST-DESC)"**, click the link in Step 1, where
you will visit a page that looks like this:

.. image:: https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/2d133c93-0ad4-4f54-997d-bb20ee5d89f1
   :width: 70%
   :alt: SLAC Identity Provider selection page

Use the text box at the bottom where it says **Enter Identity Provider Name**
to search for **SLAC Guest**.

.. image:: https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/0dbf09d9-2f74-469b-b000-a971f5453cbe
   :width: 70%
   :alt: Searching for SLAC Guest in the Identity Provider text box

Click **SLAC Guest**. This will bring up a login page:

.. image:: https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/2f98fe05-2d97-4185-baa7-211ab052abd2
   :width: 70%
   :alt: SLAC Guest login page

Click **Create Account**.

.. image:: https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/04d4aa06-d3fa-4e11-97cf-903a6a049a34
   :width: 70%
   :alt: SLAC Guest account creation form

Fill in the form, including the checkbox, and click **Create**.

.. note::

   Make sure to enter your email address correctly with no typos. You can use
   any valid email address. Preferably this should be a permanent account you
   expect to have long-term access to (such as Gmail). Your email address will
   become your SLAC Guest account username.

Once you click **Create** you should see:

.. image:: https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/0a82945a-8aab-4207-a688-f3a7acf1b06c
   :width: 70%
   :alt: Account creation confirmation screen

You will then receive an email from ``slac-iam@slac.stanford.edu`` with the
subject **"New Account Activation"**. Click the link in that email to set your
password in the web form:

.. image:: https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/4b5ff2eb-98c4-4d47-9640-cb9d54cedbe7
   :width: 70%
   :alt: Password creation form

When you have finished setting your password, click **Activate Account**. The
following screen will appear and an email from ``slac-iam@slac.stanford.edu``
with the subject **"Account Activated"** will be sent to you:

.. image:: https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1b77a5af-7c25-4fc2-977e-992f1fdbaea8
   :width: 70%
   :alt: Account activated confirmation screen

Click **Continue**. You will be brought to a login screen — enter your new SLAC
Guest account username (the email you provided when creating the account) and
the password you just set:

.. image:: https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/72e0ca01-9f57-4fdc-81f2-cae4e3df983f
   :width: 70%
   :alt: SLAC Guest account login screen

You should see the message: **"Congratulations! you have completed federated
login and Step 1 in the email instructions!"**

Your new SLAC Guest account username (the email address you provided) and your
Invitation ID should also be populated. If not, fill them in manually.

Click **Continue** to proceed with the rest of the SLAC registration process.
You will see a new confirmation message:

.. image:: https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/faf7246b-b822-4666-9c51-e6a63dab8a4c
   :width: 70%
   :alt: Post-registration confirmation screen

You will receive an email with the subject **"You have been invited to join the
SLAC Identity Portal"**, sent to the same address where you received the
original SLAC invitation.

1. Click the link in that email.

   .. image:: https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f583622e-0582-4f17-919c-721a6f3911a4
      :width: 40%
      :alt: SLAC Identity Portal invitation email

2. Click **Accept**.

   .. image:: https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/abad6390-6a9e-4fcf-8613-41b569f6a6f7
      :width: 70%
      :alt: Accept button on the SLAC Identity Portal

3. Click **Review Terms and Conditions**, read them, and click **Ok**. Then
   check **I Agree** and click **Submit**.

   You will see the following confirmation screen and receive an email with the
   subject **"Initial registration completed"**:

   .. image:: https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a8282cb6-2b25-458c-a5cf-80842ab4dc81
      :width: 70%
      :alt: Initial registration completed confirmation screen

.. note::

   Once you have created your account, it will take SLAC **up to two business
   days** to enable your access to the DESC space on SLAC Confluence. You will
   be notified by email once your Confluence access is ready.


.. _slac-guest-login:

Logging into SLAC Confluence with a SLAC Guest Account
--------------------------------------------------------

Point your web browser to:
https://confluence.slac.stanford.edu/display/LSSTDESC/Home

.. image:: https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d46358f5-16e5-461c-ae98-50e1d9d64475
   :width: 50%
   :alt: SLAC Confluence login page

1. Use the text box at the bottom where it says **Enter Identity Provider Name**
   to search for **SLAC Guest**.
2. Click **SLAC Guest**. This will bring up a login page:

   .. image:: https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1d924b8a-44b4-4e69-bda3-6ec27abb068a
      :width: 50%
      :alt: SLAC Guest login screen

3. Provide your SLAC Guest username and password and click **Login**.

4. A two-factor authentication code will be sent to the email associated with your account.


Forgotten Password for SLAC Guest Accounts
--------------------------------------------

Point your web browser to:
https://confluence.slac.stanford.edu/display/LSSTDESC/Home

.. image:: https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d46358f5-16e5-461c-ae98-50e1d9d64475
   :width: 50%
   :alt: SLAC Confluence login page

1. Use the text box at the bottom where it says **Enter Identity Provider Name**
   to search for **SLAC Guest**.
2. Click **SLAC Guest**. This will bring up a login page:

   .. image:: https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1d924b8a-44b4-4e69-bda3-6ec27abb068a
      :width: 50%
      :alt: SLAC Guest login screen

3. Click **Forgot Password?**

   .. image:: https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/2f08a89a-d60f-4921-bc14-e0477b664a62
      :width: 50%
      :alt: Forgot Password form

4. Enter your SLAC Guest account username (the email you used when creating the
   account) and click **Send Email**.
