Logging into SLAC Confluence Using Your Federated Identity
===========================================================

.. note::

   If you are a SLAC Computing account holder, please see
   `these instructions instead
   <https://github.com/LSSTDESC/slac-authentication-transition/wiki/SLAC-Computing-Accounts-(Unix-and-or-Windows-SSO)#how-to-log-into-slac-web-applications-using-your-slac-federated-identity>`_.

Once you complete the invitation process, you should test logging into SLAC
Confluence and PubDB using your federated identity credentials.

.. note::

   PubDB is updated 6 times a day with new usernames so that DESC members can
   log in. Please wait a few hours before trying to log into PubDB.

When you visit a SLAC web application such as the
`DESC space in SLAC Confluence <https://confluence.slac.stanford.edu/display/LSSTDESC/Home>`_
and click **Log in**, you will see a login screen:

.. image:: https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/552b59eb-07fb-4527-906b-b694aa98ed9f
   :width: 40%
   :alt: SLAC Confluence login screen showing the Identity Provider search box

Find your institution using the text box at the bottom where it says **Enter
Identity Provider Here**, click your institution's name, and provide your
credentials. You will use the same username and password you normally use at
your institution.

If all is well, you should be logged into SLAC Confluence and see your username
displayed in the top right of the screen.
